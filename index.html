IS-001 — Project Overview

The Problem

Veterans face a fragmented, jargon-heavy landscape of housing programs with no unified entry point. Most digital tools are program-centric, not veteran-centric. Chief Bud addresses this with a no-PII, situation-based Eligibility Snapshot that guides veterans to the right housing pathway without asking for sensitive personal information.

Research Gap

Why IS-001 Matters

No published UX studies exist on AI-powered veteran housing navigator tools specifically. IS-001 fills that gap by conducting moderated usability research on the Chief Bud Eligibility Snapshot — documenting what works, what fails, and what veteran-centric design requires in practice.
