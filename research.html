Source Selection

Sources were selected from peer-reviewed venues for relevance to at least one of three primary themes: digital navigation tools for underserved populations, human navigator effectiveness, and decision aid design for complex benefit systems. Key Themes and Findings

3 Themes
01

Digital Navigation Tools for Underserved Populations

Stafford et al. identify the floor of user expectations for digital community resource tools: fresh and accurate resource information, minimal upfront data collection, and clear next-step routing rather than overwhelming program lists.

Snapshot Implication The Snapshot must surface specific, actionable next steps — not exhaustive program directories. Minimal categorical input at intake. No PII.
02

Human Navigator Effectiveness

Kangowi et al. document the ceiling of human navigator effectiveness and supply the operational template: orient the user to what is available, surface document requirements proactively, plan against anticipated barriers, and refer to decision-making authorities rather than making determinations.

Snapshot Implication The Snapshot's output structure (orientation, document checklist, next actions, human handoff) maps directly to the Kangowi navigator model. The tool functions as a digital community health worker.
03

Decision Aid Design

Stacey et al. and the International Patient Decision Aid Standards (IPDAS) document canonical decision aid elements: presentation of options without determination, rationale for ranked pathways, explicit statement of assumptions and unknowns, non-determination disclaimer, and actionable next steps.

Snapshot Implication The Snapshot output sections correspond almost exactly to canonical IPDAS elements — enabling evaluation against an established quality standard rather than requiring a bespoke evaluation framework.

Plain Language Discipline

Regulatory text in the Snapshot output is rewritten to a sixth-to-eighth grade reading level. Acronyms are expanded on first use. Active-voice phrasing is preferred throughout. These conventions are codified in the Federal Plain Language Guidelines (PLAIN) and operationalized in the Snapshot's output style guide.

Design Implications

Operationalized in IS-001

Six design implications are directly operationalized in the Eligibility Snapshot's current output format and UI design.

1

Minimal Categorical Intake — No PII, no SSN, no claim numbers. Categorical inputs only.

2

Situation-Based Framing — Questions map to veteran life situations, not program eligibility criteria.

3

Navigator Model Output — Orient, document checklist, barrier planning, human handoff.

4

IPDAS-Compliant Structure — Ranked pathways, rationale, assumptions, disclaimer, next actions.

5

Plain Language Discipline — Grade 6-8 reading level, acronym expansion, active voice.

6

Human Handoff as Terminal Step — Every output ends with a specific named human next step.

Research Gaps

IS-001 Addresses All Three

Three gaps identified that the IS-001 capstone study is positioned to address.

△

No published UX research on veteran-specific housing navigator tools as a distinct category from general benefit navigation tools.

△

No comparative usability studies of wizard-style vs. conversational AI intake for veteran housing benefit navigation.

△

No published studies evaluating decision aid quality standards (IPDAS) applied to AI-generated veteran benefit guidance outputs.
