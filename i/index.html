Eligibility Snapshot

How the Snapshot Works

Five categorical questions. No names, no SSNs, no claim numbers — just situation-based inputs that power accurate pathway routing.

Design Principle

No PII by Design

Every input is categorical — discharge type, housing band, income bracket, dependent flag, rating tier. No personally identifiable information is collected, stored, or required. Veterans can complete the Snapshot anonymously.

Design Principle

Under Two Minutes

The five intake sections are designed for completion in 90 seconds or less. Plain-language tooltips appear on each option — not jargon. Veterans who don't know their discharge type or rating are guided forward, not blocked.

Routing Logic

Situation-First Branching

Housing status drives the primary branch. At-risk and unhoused veterans see urgency-first programs (HUD-VASH, SSVF, Emergency Vouchers) immediately. Stable veterans see goal-oriented pathways (VA Home Loan, SAH/SHA grants, tax exemptions).

Compliance

No Determinations

The Snapshot surfaces likely pathways — not eligibility decisions. Language throughout uses "may be eligible," "common requirement," and "typical process." Every pathway includes a verification step: who to contact and how to confirm.

Section Reference

Each section maps to a benefit-routing variable. Answers remain categorical throughout.

Section A
Discharge Type
Determines VA benefits eligibility floor. OTH and BCD trigger Character of Discharge review guidance. "Don't Know" proceeds with all pathways open.
Section B
Housing Status
Primary branch variable. Seven bands covering the full housing continuum — from unsheltered to stable homeowner. Drives urgency-first vs. goal-oriented routing.
Section C
Household Income
AMI band selection (Area Median Income). Used to qualify income-restricted programs such as Section 8 and SSVF rapid rehousing. No dollar amounts entered.
Section D
Dependents
Binary flag for household members who are financially supported. Affects program household size thresholds and VA loan entitlement calculations.
Section E
VA Disability Rating
Rating tier (0%, 10–40%, 50–90%, 100%/P&T) gates access to SAH/SHA adaptive housing grants, property tax exemptions, and enhanced loan terms. "No rating" is a valid answer.
Review
Review Screen
Displays all five answers before submission. Veterans can edit any answer before the Snapshot is generated. Reduces errors and increases confidence in results.
