Process Overview

Six questions. One tailored snapshot.

The Eligibility Snapshot is a single-task intake flow. A veteran answers up to six categorical questions and receives a personalized housing benefit report. It collects no personally identifiable information and makes no eligibility determinations.

The design solves a discovery problem, not a navigation one. Most veterans do not know which housing programs they may qualify for, so the flow assumes zero prerequisite knowledge: plain-language questions, tooltips that define unfamiliar terms, and an output that ends in concrete, ranked next steps. Housing status sets the path; a single conditional branch routes secure renters and owners to a goal question and everyone else to an urgency question. The answers are injected into the the Eligibility Snapshot prompt, which returns eight fixed sections from BLUF - Bottom Line Up Front, through Additional Resources.

Research Protocol

IS-001 uses a moderated think-aloud method across two rounds of usability testing.

Study Type
Moderated think-aloud usability test
Task
Complete the Eligibility Snapshot (single task)
Session Format
In-person or remote video, participant's preference
Duration
45–60 min — 5 min orientation, 25–35 min task, 10–15 min debrief

Round 1

Lo-fi / Mid-fi — Think-Aloud

3–5 participants. Focuses on flow comprehension, language clarity, and navigation assumptions. Think-aloud protocol only. No SUS.

Round 2

Hi-fi — Think-Aloud + SUS

3–5 new participants. Evaluates revised prototype after Round 1 improvements. Adds the System Usability Scale (SUS) for benchmarking.

Participant Criteria

IS-001 recruits from the veteran and veteran-services community. No PII is collected from research participants.

  • U.S. veteran (any era, any branch) OR veteran service professional (VSO counselor, case manager, housing navigator)
  • Must be 18 years of age or older
  • No PII collected — categorical inputs only throughout both test rounds
  • Participants may opt out at any point; no penalty or loss of compensation
  • Recruitment via veteran community organizations, DePaul veteran student networks, and direct outreach

Data Handling Note

No PII by Design

"IS-001 mirrors the Chief Bud product philosophy: no personally identifiable information is collected at any stage of the research. Session notes use participant codes (P01, P02…). Observation records do not capture names, addresses, or service records."
